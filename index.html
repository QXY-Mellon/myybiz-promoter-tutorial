Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free to become a Promoter?

Yes, applying and becoming a promoter is completely free. There are no fees involved.

How long does approval take?

Most applications are approved within 1-day after confirming the agreement.

How do I earn commissions?

Share your unique referral link. When someone makes a purchase through your link, you earn a commission, which is settled once per month.

Can I apply from any device?

Yes, you can register and apply from any device — desktop, tablet, or mobile phone.
